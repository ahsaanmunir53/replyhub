# ReplyHub privacy policy

> Draft. Fill in the bracketed parts and have a lawyer check it (for the UAE, against the
> Personal Data Protection Law, Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021) before charging customers.

Last updated: [date]

ReplyHub is run by [operator name], [address], contact: [email]. ReplyHub answers a
business's customers on WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger and the business's web chat, by rules
the business sets, and sends the business reports from its own website store.

## Who this covers

- **Businesses** that sign up (our customers), and their staff.
- **Their customers**, who message a business that uses ReplyHub. For their data, the
  business decides what is done with it and ReplyHub processes it on the business's behalf.

## What we keep

- **About a business:** its name, kind, country and city; the owner's name, email and
  WhatsApp number (the number is stored only as a keyed hash); the rules and answers it writes;
  its plan.
- **Chats:** the messages between the business and its customers, and the notes the assistant
  passes to the business. Customers' phone numbers are stored as keyed hashes, not in plain text.
- **Website store:** for each order, its number, time, total, currency, status, whether it is
  cash on delivery and whether it was sent out. No customer names, addresses or phone numbers.
- **Keys:** a business's WhatsApp access token and its store's read-only key, encrypted.
- **Records of changes** (an audit log), so a business can see who changed what.

We do not sell personal data and do not use it for advertising.

## Who processes it for us

- Hosting: Render (servers) and Neon (database), [regions].
- The AI model that writes replies: Meta's Muse Spark Open, run by Baseten. Messages are sent
  to it to write each reply.
- Messaging: Meta (WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger), under Meta's own terms.

## How long

Chats and store orders are kept while the business's account is open. When a business closes
its account, its data is deleted within [30] days, except what the law requires us to keep.
A customer can ask the business to delete their chats; the business can do this from its
console.

## Security

Each business's data is kept apart in the database (row-level security). Keys are encrypted.
Webhooks from Meta are checked by their signature. Store keys are read-only, and stores are
read only at public internet addresses.

## Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, for it to be corrected, or for it to be deleted: write
to [email]. A business's customers should first ask the business they messaged.

## Changes

We will post changes here and tell businesses by email before they take effect.
